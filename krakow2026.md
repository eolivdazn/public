## 🏭 Nowa Huta
The "ideal socialist city" built in the 1950s around the Lenin Steelworks: Plac Centralny, the monumental housing blocks, the steelworks gates and the Lord's Ark church.

| Option | What | Time | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Walkative — Nowa Huta walking tour](https://freewalkingtour.com/) | Guided walk, meets in the middle of Plac Centralny (yellow umbrella). Booking required. | 2h30 | Pay what you wish (most give €15–50) |
| [Crazy Guides — Communism Tour](https://www.crazyguides.com/krakow-tours/communism-tour-of-nowa-huta-district) | Private tour in a Trabant or Lada, pick-up in the Old Town, old-style restaurant stop, 1950s shop, a drink included | 2h30 | 850 PLN (≈ €200) for one person |
| [Crazy Guides — Communism Deluxe](https://www.crazyguides.com/krakow-tours/communism-deluxe-tour) | Same, plus the former steelworks headquarters | 3h30 | Ask when booking |

- **Getting there:** tram to *Plac Centralny im. R. Reagana*, about 25–30 minutes from the centre (line 4 from Teatr Bagatela / Dworzec Główny — check [Jakdojade](https://jakdojade.pl/krakow) on the day).
- **Tip:** travelling solo, the walking tour is far better value; the Trabant tour is priced per car, so it only makes sense shared.
- **After the tour:** [Restauracja Stylowa](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Restauracja%20Stylowa%20Nowa%20Huta%20Krak%C3%B3w), on the main avenue off Plac Centralny, is the classic communist-era dining room.

## 🍻 Bar hopping in Kazimierz
The old Jewish quarter is the best area for a night out: candle-lit bars with junk-shop furniture, all within a ten-minute walk. Start at Plac Wolnica and finish around Plac Nowy.

🗺️ [See the route on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Ursa%20Maior%2C%20Plac%20Wolnica%2010%2C%20Krak%C3%B3w&waypoints=Strefa%20Piwa%2C%20J%C3%B3zefa%206%2C%20Krak%C3%B3w%7CEszeweria%2C%20J%C3%B3zefa%209%2C%20Krak%C3%B3w%7COmerta%20Pub%2C%20Kupa%203%2C%20Krak%C3%B3w%7CAlchemia%2C%20Estery%205%2C%20Krak%C3%B3w%7CSinger%2C%20Estery%2020%2C%20Krak%C3%B3w&destination=Propaganda%2C%20Miodowa%2020%2C%20Krak%C3%B3w&travelmode=walking)

#### 1. Ursa Maior
📍 [Plac Wolnica 10](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ursa%20Maior%20Plac%20Wolnica%2010%20Krak%C3%B3w)

- **🍺 Drink:** their own craft beers from the brewery in the Bieszczady mountains
- **Tip:** a calm place to start, with food if you haven't eaten.

#### 2. Strefa Piwa
📍 [Józefa 6](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Strefa%20Piwa%20J%C3%B3zefa%206%20Krak%C3%B3w)

- **🍺 Drink:** 7–8 rotating taps and a big bottle list
- **Tip:** small and beer-focused — ask the bar for a recommendation.

#### 3. Eszeweria
📍 [Józefa 9](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Eszeweria%20J%C3%B3zefa%209%20Krak%C3%B3w)

- **🍷 Drink:** anything — you come for the candles, antiques and the hidden entrance
- **Tip:** the most atmospheric room of the night.

#### 4. Omerta Pub
📍 [Kupa 3](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Omerta%20Pub%20Kupa%203%20Krak%C3%B3w)

- **🍺 Drink:** about 30 taps, mostly small Polish breweries
- **Tip:** the main craft-beer stop of the route.

#### 5. Plac Nowy — zapiekanka break
📍 [Okrąglak, Plac Nowy](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Okr%C4%85glak%20Plac%20Nowy%20Krak%C3%B3w)

- **🍽 Eat:** zapiekanka (toasted open baguette with mushrooms and cheese) from the windows of the round building
- **Tip:** open late — the standard mid-crawl stop.

#### 6. Alchemia
📍 [Estery 5](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Alchemia%20Estery%205%20Krak%C3%B3w)

- **🍸 Drink:** beer or a cocktail in one of the four candle-lit rooms
- **Tip:** the Kazimierz classic; often has concerts in the cellar.

#### 7. Singer
📍 [Estery 20](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Singer%20Estery%2020%20Krak%C3%B3w)

- **🍸 Drink:** vodka or beer at a sewing-machine table
- **Tip:** gets lively late, with dancing in the back room.

#### 8. Propaganda
📍 [Miodowa 20](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Propaganda%20Miodowa%2020%20Krak%C3%B3w)

- **🍺 Drink:** cheap beer and shots among communist-era memorabilia
- **Tip:** loud, punk-rock and open until the early hours — a good last stop.
